CATEGORY: Shooting (alternative: Action)

TAGS (max 5): Action, Shooting, War, Survival, Boat

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Command a lone gunboat against an island fortress chain. Smash coastal bases, sink patrol fleets, rescue prisoners under lighthouse beams, land marines and escort transports through minefields — every mission is generated fresh, and every fifth one sends an enemy flagship (LEVIATHAN, TEMPEST or FORTRESS) to kill you.

The campaign never ends, it only gets harder. Earn credits, fit 12 boat upgrades (homing rockets, CIWS, radar, depth charges), spin the supply drum, collect the daily ration and 3 daily orders, and climb 10 naval ranks.

Endless campaign • 4 mission types + boss fights • day/night, storms, submarines and waterspouts • 9 languages • keyboard + mouse + touch.

=== CONTROLS ===
KEYBOARD AND MOUSE
WASD / arrows — steer the boat
Mouse — aim, Left click — main gun, Right click — homing rockets
SHIFT — afterburner, F — smoke screen, Q — depth charges
E — arsenal (next to the carrier), H — headquarters
P — pause, O — settings, M — sound

TOUCH
Left half of the screen — steering stick
Right half — aim and fire
Buttons on the right: afterburner, rocket, smoke, depth charges, arsenal

Your objective is printed at the top of the screen. Carry rescued people aboard and hand them over to the carrier — the boat has a limited number of seats. Targets destroyed back to back pay more credits, and credits are spent in the arsenal between missions.
